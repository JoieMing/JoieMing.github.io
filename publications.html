Cooperative Model Caching and Network Slicing in AI-RAN-Assisted Vehicular Networks

Yujun Ming, Boyuan Qu, Ke Zhang, Supeng Leng

Submitted to IEEE Transactions on Cognitive Communications and Networking

Proposes CLRO, a dual-timescale cross-layer framework for reliable AI-model delivery in vehicular AI-RANs. Hetero-QMIX jointly learns long-term inter-slice resource reservation and cache placement, while iterative SCA optimizes short-term intra-slice resource allocation and coordinated beamforming, reducing system cost by up to 70%.

Tiered Digital Twin-Assisted Cooperative Multiple Targets Tracking

Longyu Zhou, Supeng Leng, Qing Wang, Yujun Ming, Qiang Liu

IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, vol. 23, no. 4, pp. 3749–3762, 2024

Designs a tiered digital twin-assisted framework for real-time, accurate multi-target tracking with UAV swarms. Coarse-grained imitation supports robust target coverage, while fine-grained imitation combines trajectory prediction with a reaction-diffusion mechanism to select feasible cooperative UAVs.
